1. Description of Service

Mailgrant provides selective, zero-trust email proxying and access control. It enables mailbox owners to connect authorized email accounts (such as Google Gmail or Microsoft Outlook) and issue revocable, time-bound, and scope-restricted access keys to recipients for specific verification codes, magic sign-in links, or approved messages without disclosing email passwords or full inbox contents.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Mailgrant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, personalized advertising, or training artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Sharing Keys and Bearer Access

Mailgrant access keys operate as scoped bearer credentials. Anyone possessing an active key can view emails matching that key's assigned permissions. You agree to distribute keys only to intended, trusted recipients and to promptly revoke keys upon task completion. Mailgrant is not liable for unauthorized access resulting from user negligence in key transmission.

5. Ephemeral Retention & Deletion

Email content processed by Mailgrant is stored ephemerally with AES-GCM encryption and is subject to automated purging based on the retention window configured by the workspace owner (defaulting to 15 minutes to 24 hours). Mailgrant is an access delegation tool, not a permanent email archiving or backup system.
