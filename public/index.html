How Mailgrant Works in 3 Steps

From full Gmail isolation to granular code sharing in less than 2 minutes.

STEP 01

Connect Your Mailbox

Sign in with your Google account. Mailgrant connects with read-only Gmail access without requiring you to purchase a custom domain.

STEP 02

Whitelist Approved Senders

Create exact sender rules (e.g. *@mail.anthropic.com). Choose whether to extract 6-digit codes only, magic sign-in links, or plain text.

STEP 03

Share Revocable Keys

Copy an access key and send it privately. Recipients paste the key once at /access.html and view their codes. Revoke anytime in one click.

Security Architecture

Built on Zero-Trust Principles

Every boundary is enforced on the server. Your Google password and sensitive personal emails are never exposed.

Zero Password Sharing

Never give away your Gmail credentials. Recipients only receive temporary, scoped access tokens managed by the proxy.

Strict Sender Whitelisting

Only matching authenticated emails pass through. Bank alerts, personal correspondence, and unapproved services are rejected at the edge.

Code-Only Retention

For 2FA verification rules, the application strips out the entire email body and retains only the extracted OTP code digits.

Automatic Expiry

Messages automatically expire after 15 to 60 minutes. Once expired, message content is completely inaccessible.

Multi-Mailbox Isolation

Manage personal, work, and family Gmail accounts in isolated workspaces under a single owner dashboard.

30-Day Activity Audit

Every key redemption, permission update, and message open is logged in a tamper-resistant activity ledger without storing sensitive bodies.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and using Mailgrant.

Do recipients need a Google account or password?

No! Recipients never need a Google account, password, or third-party sign-in. You simply generate an access key, and they paste it at /access.html.

Can Mailgrant send or delete emails in my Gmail?

No. Mailgrant only requests read-only search permission. It has zero capability to send emails, delete messages, or alter your account settings.

What happens when I revoke an access key?

Revocation is immediate. The moment you revoke a key in your dashboard, all remembered browser devices holding that key are locked out permanently.

How does the interactive demo work?

The demo runs completely in your browser local storage using simulated messages. You can test adding rules, creating keys, and viewing codes without connecting any real accounts.
