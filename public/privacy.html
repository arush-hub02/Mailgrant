Privacy and Data Controls

Zero-trust architectural boundaries and data retention policies

AES-GCM Protected

Mailgrant lets mailbox owners authorize Google access and share selected email content with people they choose. The deployment operator is responsible for the application and provides the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.

Google Data & OAuth Permissions

Basic sign-in uses your verified Google identity. Connecting each Gmail is a separate read-only authorization. Google grants mailbox-wide read permission; Mailgrant searches approved senders and enforces its sender, subject, time and recipient checks before retaining content. The backend can process Gmail data and is not end-to-end encrypted.

What Is Stored

Account identifiers, workspace settings, sender rules, permissions, sharing-key hashes, protected session records and activity metadata are stored. Gmail refresh tokens and retained email content are encrypted using the deployment's protected encryption key (AES-GCM). Codes-only rules retain extracted codes; full-reading rules retain plain text. Attachments and active HTML are not served.

Sharing & Retention

Owners choose sharing permissions, expiry and limits. Anyone holding a bearer access key can use its permissions. Keys can be revoked. Retained messages expire according to each rule, with a default of 15 minutes, and activity history is retained for 30 days. Expired content cannot be read while awaiting scheduled deletion. Provider backups have their own retention.

Owner & Recipient Controls

Disconnect a Gmail to remove its stored credential. Revoke the Google grant through Google Account connections to stop provider access. Delete retained messages or the workspace in Settings. Delete a workspace to remove its rules, keys, memberships, credentials and retained mail. Basic sign-in account metadata may remain until removed by the deployment operator; contact the support address shown in the Google consent screen for account-level deletion.

Recipients may forget one saved key or all saved access on their device. Revocation cannot erase copies already made or end sessions in other services.

Service Providers

Google provides identity and Gmail APIs. Cloudflare hosts the live application and database. Mail data is used to provide the owner's requested sharing features. This application does not include advertising, third-party tracking scripts, or sale of mail data.
